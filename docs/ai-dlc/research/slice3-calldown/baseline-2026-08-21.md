# Post-slice-2 baseline for slice 3 (calldown) — measurement report

**Bottom line: the roster is still 7–35 percentage points (pp) above every
grounded went-to-showdown (WTSD — the fraction of hands that see a flop and
ride to a showdown instead of folding along the way) band, in the same
direction on both measuring instruments. `nit`, `tag`, and `lag` are furthest
off; `passive_fish` is closest.** Measured at commit `d351150`
(`d351150bfff8bc3d9d2bd4b9d9fb687c9b1c9de9`, origin/main tip fetched
2026-08-21), on the ratified nine-seat lineup (the fixed table every
flywheel measurement uses for comparability), seed 20260817, in an isolated
worktree — no repo files were changed by the measurement itself. This is the
baseline `docs/ai-dlc/specs/flywheel-slice3-calldown.md` measures its
tickets against.

The two slice-2 counters (invest-then-fold events, the all-in-hand share)
moved a little since slice 2 closed but the air-only deterministic-river-fold
count is unchanged at 144. Nothing here reads as a defect in the measurement
itself — every number matches the shape the last baseline described, just at
a fresh tip.

lineup; the band harness (`_persona_stats` in the test file) plays a
different fixed sample at its own pinned seed and is the one slice 3 is

## 5. Residual deterministic river folds (air-only)

**144** — unchanged from the last-quoted baseline. This is the count of
river folds where the hand class is `air|none` (no draw), the seat has
already committed at least 25bb, is facing pot odds of 1/6 or worse, AND the
call would need at least the entire remaining stack — the cell where
`personas_postflop.py:1010` hard-zeroes call merit, so FOLD is the only
weighted action (P(fold)=1.000, not a mixed strategy). The same restriction
to `ace_high|none` reads 302 (`diagnose.py` bundles both into its printed
"446" combined figure, so this air-only split required a small supplementary
script, not a plain `diagnose.py` read).

## Anomalies / things worth a second look

- **987 vs 1,015** for invest-then-fold events is the one number that moved
  outside plausible seed noise since slice 2 closed. Worth confirming this
  repo state (post PR #206, "thread opponents through the price-measurement
  fixtures") is expected to have shifted it, or whether it's incidental drift
  from an engine change in between.
- Everything else (144 air-only residual, all-in share, per-persona AF/FtC
  bands) landed close enough to the previously quoted figures to read as
  normal seed/tip variance, not a regression.
