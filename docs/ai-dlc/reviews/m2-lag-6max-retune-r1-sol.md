## Bottom line

**Verdict: fail.** The board-made-hand rule, override merge, and raise-multiple calculation are supported by the code. The 9-max attribution check is unsound because the golden harness shares an RNG across hands. The spec also requires three commits while scheduling four, and several pass/fail checks can accept results outside their stated targets.
